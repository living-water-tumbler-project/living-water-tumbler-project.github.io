# Living Water Tumblers

Living Water Tumblers is a ministry in Bonifacio Global City. We give traffic enforcers a drink tumbler marked with Scripture. The tumbler is a gift: encouragement in the hand, and an invitation toward Jesus and a local church.

The name comes from John 4:13–14. At the well, Jesus tells the Samaritan woman that ordinary water leaves a person thirsty again, and that the water he gives becomes a spring of eternal life.

## The gift

- A customized insulated tumbler, printed with a Bible verse
- Given as a gift, bagged so it is clearly a gift
- Accompanied by an invitation card centered on John 4:13–14

The first run was at night on 22 December 2025. The work continues.

## This site

This repository is the public home for the project, published with GitHub Pages at [living-water-tumbler-project.github.io](https://living-water-tumbler-project.github.io/).

It is where we keep what the ministry is, and where a printed link or QR code on the gift can land.
